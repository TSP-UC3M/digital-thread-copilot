## Rules for the slides

- One message per slide, stated in its title.
- Every number on a slide is copied from a page of this site and names it.
- The demonstration follows a written script and has a recorded backup.
- The slides, the script, the backup video and the question sheet are stored in this repository under `docs/defense/`.
