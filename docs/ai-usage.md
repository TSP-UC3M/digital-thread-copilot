## Data sent to third-party AI services

On 2026-11-16, records exported from IEEE Xplore and Scopus were given to third-party AI services: the three full exports and the screening sheet to GitHub Copilot for the rejected cross-check, and the screening file to Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google) for the assisted screening. The data concerned are bibliographic records with their abstracts (64 records, 53 unique); no full text was sent, and the records were not republished: only reduced exports without abstracts are in this repository (see `references/search-2026-11-16/README.md`).

We read the published terms afterwards, on 2026-11-17. The terms of Elsevier for Scopus admit the use of an AI tool only in a closed environment that does not train an external tool and does not share any part of Scopus with a third party. The licence terms of IEEE also restrict the use of its content with AI tools; we could not establish from the published texts whether they cover the analysis of a few abstracts. The licence signed by UC3M, which may differ, was not read. Whether this limited use was permitted therefore remains unclear to us, and for Scopus it was probably not. The group stopped the practice on 2026-11-17: no export and no full text is given to a third-party AI service.
